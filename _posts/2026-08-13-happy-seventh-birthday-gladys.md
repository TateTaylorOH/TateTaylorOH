Today Gladys turns seven! It is crazy to think that when I first published [Gladys the Corgi](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/50164) she was only one year old, how the time flies.

To celebrate a bit, I've just published a new update for Gladys! Carys has received a complete revoice with the help of the very talented [Elizabeth Plant](https://www.elizabethplant.com/) and I have added several more lines to improve Carys's interactivity with the world around her (combat lines, theft lines, ect). I have also updated Gladys's [Dragonborn Bestiary](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/123521) entry to be consistent with the Elder Scrolls Online's new [corgi lore](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dawngold_Corgi) ([Merlin](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/56433)'s entry was also updated in his own mod). Lastly, and perhaps most importantly, I have finally ESLified Gladys!

That's all for now, hope you enjoy the little update!
